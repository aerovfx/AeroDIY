# Long Range UAV — reference analysis

Suitability: **conditional pass**. The 1344×768 image has one clear hard-surface vehicle, a strong three-quarter silhouette, readable major materials, and enough visible structure for a procedural browser model. Only one view exists, so the dorsal rear, port side, underside and exact airfoil sections remain inferred; this is a visual reconstruction, not manufacturing geometry.

## Observation

- Classification: unmanned fixed-wing aircraft; hard-surface mechanical object; confidence 0.98.
- Overall form: bilateral aircraft with a long tapered fuselage, extremely high-aspect-ratio straight wing, and twin canted tail planes. The wing span is roughly 2.7× the visible fuselage length.
- Macro assemblies: fuselage shell, main wing, twin tail, dorsal engine pod, under-wing engine nacelle, landing gear, ventral payload bay and nose sensor turret.
- Meso assemblies: rounded nose fairing, stepped lower fuselage, wingtip caps/navigation lamps, turbine inlet and fan, dorsal intake, access panels, antennas and control-surface seams.
- Micro groups: panel fasteners, pitot/sensor marks, turbine blades, gimbal lens, red starboard navigation lamp, hinge/seam lines.
- Spatial relationships: wing intersects the upper-mid fuselage; nacelle hangs below the visible wing on a short pylon; sensor turret sockets into the nose underside; landing strut sockets into the lower mid-fuselage; twin tail planes overlap the aft dorsal body and cant outward.
- Materials: primary shell is light gray painted composite (dielectric, satin, roughness about 0.42); inlet lips and struts are darker gray painted metal/composite; lenses are dark glass with low roughness; tires are matte rubber; fan hub/blades are dark metal.
- Finish/color: low-saturation cool gray shell with subtle panel-to-panel value shifts; no strong decals; red navigation emitter on the visible wingtip.
- Identity features: very long wing, bulbous sensor nose, asymmetric visible under-wing turbofan, dorsal rear intake/pod, twin outward-canted tail, and long single landing strut.

## Inference and uncertainty

- The hidden port-side nacelle is inferred for symmetry; the image only proves the visible starboard nacelle.
- Airfoil thickness, port panel layout, rear exhaust details and complete landing-gear layout are undetermined.
- The tail is interpreted as a symmetric V-tail. Exact hinge topology is inferred from faint seams.
- Dimensions are normalized from the image silhouette and are not engineering measurements.

## Quality contract

The model must read immediately as this reference at card size and at a three-quarter review angle. Blocking failures are: short/stubby wing, generic ellipsoid fuselage, missing nose gimbal, absent nacelle/fan, conventional vertical tail replacing the canted pair, floating attachments, or toy-like high-gloss materials. It should remain explodable/clickable through named groups and expose useful animation pivots for turbine fans and sensor gimbal.
